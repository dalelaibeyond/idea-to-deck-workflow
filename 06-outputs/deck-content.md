    * **Declining gross margin:** Integrator gross margins fell from 41.9% to 38.6% in one year.
    * **Equipment-bound revenue:** 76% of installation revenue is tied to hardware, not services.
    * **The verdict:** "Resale stopped working as a standalone business." — DQ Channels
* Presenter Delivery:
  - Voiceover Script: "Let's be direct about the problem. The hardware business that built ICS is no longer a source of durable profit. And here is the structural trap: 76 percent of our installation revenue is still tied to equipment, not to the services and expertise where the value now lives. Every major industry observer says the same thing. Resale, on its own, no longer works as a business model. This is not a cyclical dip. This is a permanent reset. And it affects us directly."
  - Delivery Mindset: Gravitas and directness. Slow down on "permanent reset." Let the numbers land before moving forward.
