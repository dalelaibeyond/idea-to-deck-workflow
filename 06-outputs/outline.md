# Executive Narrative (SCQA Framework)
* **Situation:** ICS is a 48-year-old traditional ICT system integrator serving the Philippines' top 1,000 enterprises, with ~10% revenue growth in 2024. The business is anchored on hardware resale (Dell, HP, Lenovo) and legacy services.
* **Complication:** Hardware margins have structurally collapsed — 84% of integrators report margins at or below 30%, servers and storage are in single digits. The resale model "stopped working as a standalone business." Without AI capability, ICS risks losing its advisory role with top-1,000 clients as AI-native competitors enter the market.
* **Question:** How does ICS transform from a hardware-dependent SI into an AI-first system integrator — before the window of first-mover advantage closes in the Philippines?
* **Answer:** Build an AI Agent delivery practice now, leveraging ICS's 40+ years of customer trust, deep domain expertise in IT audit/SAP/MDR, and the mature Microsoft and Salesforce AI Agent ecosystems — turning shrinking hardware margins into growing services revenue.
